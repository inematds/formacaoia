What you’ll know how to do

Results, not theory

Each lesson ends with something written, yours, and ready to use on Monday. By the end of the course, these pieces form a single document: your work structure.

A request in five parts — role, task, format, constraints, and completion criteria, instead of the loose sentence you write today.

A one-page dossier — what the AI needs to know about your work, written once and pasted every time.

A fixed recipe — the task you repeat most, turned into steps someone else can follow.

A memory card — what carries over from one conversation to another, without saving anything sensitive.

Three levels of limits — what’s allowed, what requires your authorization, and what’s prohibited, in writing.

One three-minute review cycle — the step that separates “the AI is finished” from “the work is ready.”

Who it’s for

Made for where you are right now

is for you if

  • You already use chat AI at work and feel like you’ve hit a ceiling on quality.
  • You’re a lawyer, marketing manager, accountant, teacher — someone who produces text and makes decisions, not code.
  • You want to stop re-explaining the same thing every week and manually checking everything just to be safe.
  • You have 15 to 20 minutes per session and prefer to leave each lesson with something ready.

isn’t for you if

  • You’re looking for technical content about programming or building systems.
  • You want a list of this month’s tools and shortcuts — the focus here is on what remains useful as the tools change.
  • You prefer video lessons to reading at your own pace.
